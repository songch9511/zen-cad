# Common Families

This file lists interface facts commonly needed for first-pass layout. It is not a catalog.

## Motors

For a motor layout, capture:

- mounting face;
- shaft axis;
- shaft diameter and protrusion assumption;
- bolt pattern;
- pilot boss diameter if relevant;
- body envelope;
- cable/connector keep-out.

## Belts And Pulleys

For a belt layout, capture:

- belt pitch;
- belt width;
- belt mid-plane;
- pulley axis;
- pitch diameter or tooth count when known;
- bore/shaft interface;
- center distance;
- idler/tensioner strategy.

## Bearings And Shafts

Capture:

- shaft axis;
- bore diameter;
- outer diameter or seat envelope;
- width;
- axial retention assumptions;
- clearance or press-fit intent.

## Rails And Sliders

Capture:

- travel axis;
- rail datum face;
- carriage envelope;
- mounting hole pattern or slots;
- travel limits;
- clearance envelope.

## Screws And Fasteners

Capture:

- screw family;
- clearance hole diameter;
- threaded hole or insert assumption;
- head clearance/counterbore if needed;
- axis and pattern positions.

## Gears

Capture:

- module or pitch;
- tooth count;
- pitch diameter;
- shaft axis;
- mesh center distance;
- mesh plane;
- intended ratio.
