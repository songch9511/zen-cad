# Lessons: Flat Planetary Gear Assembly

## What Failed

1. The first interpretation of "teeth must not overlap" was too shallow.
   - Failure: the model avoided tooth overlap by effectively separating or loosely aligning teeth.
   - Why it was wrong: in a planetary gear, teeth must intermesh into opposing tooth spaces while solid tooth volumes remain non-overlapping.
   - Lesson: "no overlap" is not the same as "no contact" or "visually separated."

2. The ring gear was not treated as a real internal gear at first.
   - Failure: the outside ring looked like a toothed ring, but the planets did not convincingly mesh with its internal teeth.
   - Why it was wrong: a planetary ring gear must satisfy the same module/pitch system as the sun and planets.
   - Correct relation used later: `Nr = Ns + 2Np`, with `Ns/Np/Nr = 24/18/60`, `module = 2 mm`, and planet center radius `42 mm`.

3. Simplified radial trapezoid teeth broke down at the internal ring mesh.
   - Failure: radial block teeth could be phased to work at one mesh line, but neighboring teeth caused flank interference around the internal ring.
   - Why it was wrong: internal gear meshing is not just radial slots; the flank geometry matters.
   - Lesson: for planetary gears, especially internal ring meshes, use involute geometry or a proven gear generator before claiming engineering plausibility.

4. Visual review was initially over-weighted.
   - Failure: screenshots made the layout look acceptable before the actual mesh geometry was sufficiently checked.
   - Correct approach: use Boolean common volume checks between every meshing pair.
   - Required checks: sun/planet for all three planets and planet/ring for all three planets.

5. The first "premium" pass risked being cosmetic only.
   - User correctly asked for fillets and more mature detail because the model still looked toy-like.
   - Improvement had to preserve mesh facts while adding non-mesh detail.

## What The User Asked To Correct

1. "톱니들이 겹치면 안됨."
   - Interpreted first as simple non-overlap.
   - Corrected meaning: teeth must mesh, but solid tooth bodies must not occupy the same volume.

2. "가장 큰 바깥 Gear랑도 잘 맞물려야지."
   - Required the ring gear to be treated as an internal gear, not a decorative outer toothed circle.

3. "Planetary Gear에 대해서 더 조사하고 생성할 수 없어?"
   - Required using planetary gear relationships instead of hand-tuned visual placement.

4. "fillet 등을 넣어서 좀 장난감스럽지 않게, 더 고급지게."
   - Required a detail pass: root fillets, hub bosses, ring lips, mount holes, carrier holes, and pin caps.

## What Improved

1. The gear train was rebuilt around planetary constraints.
   - `Ns = 24`
   - `Np = 18`
   - `Nr = 60`
   - `module = 2 mm`
   - `sun pitch radius = 24 mm`
   - `planet pitch radius = 18 mm`
   - `ring pitch radius = 60 mm`
   - `24 + 18 = 42`
   - `60 - 18 = 42`

2. The tooth geometry moved from trapezoid proxy to involute geometry.
   - Sun and planets use `bd_warehouse.gear.SpurGear`.
   - Ring gear is generated by subtracting a scaled 60T involute cutter from the ring blank.
   - Ring cutter scale `1.01x` provides static tooth-space clearance/backlash.

3. Mesh phase was validated, not guessed.
   - Sun rotation: `0 deg`
   - Planet rotations: `theta - 10 deg`
   - Ring slot phase: `-3 deg`
   - Result: all six meshing pairs have Boolean common volume `0.0`.

4. The final model gained premium non-mesh details.
   - `0.1 mm` tooth root fillets.
   - Raised sun and planet hub bosses.
   - Ring outer lips.
   - Six ring mount holes.
   - Carrier edge fillet and six lightening holes.
   - Filleted pin stems and caps.

5. The validation artifacts became more meaningful.
   - STEP generation report.
   - Supplemental geometry report.
   - Viewer snapshot.
   - Layout snapshot.
   - Proceed gate and review bundle.

## Rules For Next Time

1. Do not generate a planetary gear assembly from visual intuition alone.
   - First lock tooth counts, module, pitch radii, center distance, and planet spacing condition.

2. Do not use radial trapezoid teeth for an internal ring gear if the user expects engineering plausibility.
   - Trapezoid teeth are acceptable only as a clearly labeled rough visual proxy.
   - For internal ring mesh, use involute geometry or a negative cutter workflow.

3. Do not treat "teeth do not overlap" as "teeth are separated."
   - Correct criterion: teeth enter opposing tooth spaces while Boolean common volume remains zero.

4. Do not approve a ring gear by screenshot.
   - Must check planet/ring Boolean intersections for all planets.

5. Do not add fillets/details before mesh validation is stable.
   - Detail pass must be non-mesh and must not alter pitch radii, gear centers, tooth counts, or phase.

6. Do not let cosmetic improvements hide drivetrain errors.
   - Premium details are secondary to the locked gear mesh.

7. Do not claim production readiness from this STEP.
   - Static CAD validation is not torque, load, wear, lubrication, backlash dynamics, noise, or manufacturability certification.

## Reusable Checklist

- Confirm `Nr = Ns + 2Np`.
- Confirm `planet_radius = module * (Ns + Np) / 2`.
- Confirm `ring_pitch_radius - planet_pitch_radius = planet_radius`.
- Confirm equal planet spacing condition, e.g. `(Ns + Nr) / planet_count` is an integer for this sign convention.
- Use involute sun and planet teeth.
- Generate internal ring as a negative involute cutter or equivalent internal gear profile.
- Phase sun, planets, and ring slots.
- Check Boolean common volume for every sun/planet and planet/ring pair.
- Only then add fillets, hubs, lips, holes, caps, and other premium details.
- Re-run STEP import, bbox, volume, snapshot, and proceed gate packaging.

## Final Session Outcome

The final assembly uses module-locked involute gears with a negative-cutter internal ring, premium non-mesh details, and zero Boolean common volume across all six meshing pairs. It is ready for user review as a static CAD review model, not as a certified production gearset.
